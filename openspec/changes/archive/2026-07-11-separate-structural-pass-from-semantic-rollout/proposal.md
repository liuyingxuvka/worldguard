## Why

WorldGuard can currently report success for structurally present child models even when their semantic equations or transition rules are unevaluable or contradictory. Structural contract health and semantic world simulation must be separate, honest claims.

## What Changes

- Add explicit structural, semantic, provider, and rollout status fields with fail-closed aggregation.
- Treat missing evaluators, unavailable providers, incomplete BDI/RCC8/resource/causal/conflict/norm semantics, and unevaluable equations as visible non-pass states.
- Add true input/output bindings for minimal target-owned semantic child executors.
- Add negative probes for the previously accepted shallow cases and a WorldGuard depth receipt.
- Bind SkillGuard to WorldGuard's original model-mesh route instead of creating a parallel simulator.
- Adopt the repository with a generated SkillGuard project-maintenance block that preserves WorldGuard child semantics.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `semantic-rollout-status`: Honest separation and aggregation of structural contract checks, semantic execution, provider availability, and depth evidence.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

WorldGuard contracts, child-model adapters, mesh evaluation, reports, skill prompts, fixtures, negative probes, and tests are affected. Domain semantics stay owned by WorldGuard children.
